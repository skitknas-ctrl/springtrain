How to play

  • Right lever = power, left lever = brake. Slide a lever up and hold it. Let go and it drops back to zero.
  • Drive the track five laps as fast as you can. The clock starts at GO.
  • The engine and the wagons are joined by springs. Only the engine has brakes, so when you brake the heavy wagons keep pushing from behind, and when you accelerate they lag and then snap forward.
  • Watch the curve load bar. If any part of the train takes a curve too fast it derails and the run is over. A wagon can derail even when the engine made it through.
  • The small map shows the whole track. Tight curves are marked in orange and red.
  • Finish to earn coins. Faster times give more stars and more coins. Spend them in the shop on a stronger engine, better brakes or a new train.
  • Later tracks add more wagons and hills. Uphill costs speed, downhill the wagons push harder.
